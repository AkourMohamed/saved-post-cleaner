Saved Post Cleaner — Settings

These control how fast the automation runs on your Instagram Saved page and how it paces itself. The Recommended values (use "Restore recommended defaults" below) are a reasonable balance; you can override any of them, but faster settings increase the chance Instagram temporarily rate-limits the action on your account. Changes apply the next time you load or reload your Saved page.

Pacing between posts

Randomized between these two values before every post — never a fixed interval, which is itself something anti-automation checks look for.

Cooldown break

A longer randomized pause every N unsaves, on top of the per-post delay above. Turning this off makes runs noticeably more bot-like.

Session limit

Once reached, the panel pauses and you click Start again to continue — keeps an unattended run from going for hours straight.
